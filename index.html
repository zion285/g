a little universe, just for you

to my moon, anis ♡

a little collection of love, prayers, and reminders for your heart
🌙

hey, my favourite person.

i made this little space just for you. nothing complicated, nothing you need to figure out. just a few words, some reminders from the Qur'an, and little prayers that i hope will bring you comfort whenever you need them. so take your time, read slowly, and remember that you deserve kindness too. ♡
✦ ♡ ✦
☁️

please take it slowly.

you don't have to figure everything out in one day. some days will be good, some days might be tiring, and some days you might just want to rest. that's okay. you are a human being, not a machine. you don't have to be productive every second to be worthy of something good. please be gentle with yourself, anis.
♡
📖

Allah knows your limits.

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا
“Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear.”
SURAH AL-BAQARAH · 2:286
when life feels difficult, remember that you can ask Allah for help. you don't have to carry every worry by yourself.
✨

hard times don't last forever.

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا
“For indeed, with hardship comes ease. Indeed, with hardship comes ease.”
SURAH ASH-SHARH · 94:5–6
i pray that even on your hardest days, Allah lets you find little reasons to keep going and gives your heart some peace.
🤲

when you feel overwhelmed.

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي ۝ وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي
“My Lord, expand my chest and make my task easy for me.”
SURAH TA-HA · 20:25–26
you can say this dua before studying, before an important moment, or whenever your heart feels heavy. may Allah make things easier for you.
🌱

keep learning, one step at a time.

وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
“And say, ‘My Lord, increase me in knowledge.’”
SURAH TA-HA · 20:114
before you study, take a breath and make this dua. you don't have to understand everything immediately. learning takes time, and every little effort counts.
🌌

your effort matters.

وَأَن لَّيْسَ لِلْإِنسَانِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ
“And that each person will only have what they endeavoured towards.”
SURAH AN-NAJM · 53:39
don't compare your progress with someone else's. focus on what you can do today. even a small effort is still an effort, and you can be proud of trying.
🕊️

give your heart a moment.

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ
“Surely in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find comfort.”
SURAH AR-RA'D · 13:28
when your mind gets too noisy, take a little break. make dhikr, pray, or sit quietly for a while. you deserve a moment to breathe.
⭐

put your trust in Allah.

وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ
“And whoever puts their trust in Allah, then He is sufficient for them.”
SURAH AT-TALAQ · 65:3
do your best, make dua, and leave what you cannot control to Allah. you don't need to know every answer right now.
🌷

Allah sees the good you do.

فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ
“So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it.”
SURAH AZ-ZALZALAH · 99:7
even the little things you do when nobody notices matter. keep trying to do good, even in the smallest ways.
🌤️

don't lose hope.

لَا تَقْنَطُوا مِن رَّحْمَةِ اللَّهِ
“Do not despair of the mercy of Allah.”
SURAH AZ-ZUMAR · 39:53
if something doesn't go as planned, it doesn't mean everything is over. you can start again, ask Allah for guidance, and try once more.
💜

you can ask Allah for help.

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
“You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.”
SURAH AL-FATIHAH · 1:5
you don't need perfect words when making dua. speak honestly to Allah about what you're worried about and what you're hoping for.
🌙

Allah is aware of you.

وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كُنتُمْ
“And He is with you wherever you are.”
SURAH AL-HADID · 57:4
wherever life takes you, remember that Allah knows what you're going through. may remembering Him bring peace to your heart.
🌸

a dua for your studies.

ya Allah, please bless anis with beneficial knowledge, a calm heart, and a clear mind. make her studies easier, help her understand what she learns, and bless her time and effort. when she feels nervous, give her confidence. when she feels tired, give her strength. when she feels lost, guide her towards what is good. ameen ya rabbal alamin. 🤍
🤲 ♡
💫

you don't have to be perfect.

i hope you know that you don't have to be perfect for me to be proud of you. you can have a bad day. you can be tired. you can need time for yourself. i want you to take care of your studies, your health, your prayers, and yourself too. please don't feel guilty for needing rest. you're allowed to slow down.
♡
🪐

you deserve peace, too.

i never want the people who care about you to become another source of stress in your life. take care of your responsibilities, give yourself time to grow, and don't feel like you have to be available every second. the people who genuinely care about you should want to see you doing well, too.
you deserve understanding and kindness ♡
🌠

a little prayer for you.

may Allah protect you wherever you go, guide your decisions, bless your friendships, and keep you safe. may He put barakah in your time, ease your worries, and let your hard work lead you towards something good. may your heart find peace in the moments you need it most. ameen ya Allah. 🤍
🤲 ✨
💌

one last thing, anis.

i'm rooting for you, always. keep learning, keep making dua, and keep believing that better days are possible. you don't have to have everything figured out right now. just take the next step, then the next one. i hope this little corner of the universe reminds you to be patient with yourself and to keep Allah close to your heart. you've got this. one day at a time. ♡
with love, always ♡
